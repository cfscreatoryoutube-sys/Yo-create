Meet the Slinger

Our first prototype is a launcher for the Teen box. Load a rubber band, aim and fling.

This is an early version, not the finished product, so the final design may change. Because it launches objects, an adult should always supervise.

Warning!

Not suitable for children under 13. Never point at people, animals or faces. Keep away from eyes. Use only the projectiles supplied. Check the rubber band before each use and stop using it if it’s worn or damaged. An adult should be supervising.
