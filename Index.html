Our first prototypes

Two early builds, one for each box. Neither is the finished product, so the final designs may change.

Teen box, ages 13+

The rubber band car

Pull the car back and the rubber bands load up. Let go and it drives forward. Every Teen box toy comes with a simple explanation of the science, like how stretched rubber bands store energy.

Kids box, ages 6-12

The build car

A car you build yourself, then roll and play with. A hands-on mechanical build, with no flying.

Warning!

Teen box: not suitable for children under 13. Adult supervision recommended. Keep fingers and faces clear when pulling the car back. Don’t release it towards people or animals. Check the rubber bands before each use and stop using them if they’re worn or damaged.

Before any box ships

  • The final designs get rounded corners and edges.
  • Each toy goes through a safety assessment and testing.
  • It carries the required UK safety marking.
  • The warning and instructions go on the box and in the box.

Who makes it

Squeeble designs and models every toy, which can take hours to get right for just one box. Cole is the co-owner and media manager. He writes everything online and runs the website, Reddit and YouTube.
